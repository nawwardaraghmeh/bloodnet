### Prompt

> Given the same specifications model, annotate the classes with JPA annotations that map them
> on a relational database. consider the following requirements:
> All five associations are bidirectional: annotate the many side as @ManyToOne and the one side
> as @OneToMany with mappedBy;
> Annotate enumerated attributes with @Enumerated(EnumType.STRING);
> Use field annotations and generated primary keys;
> Keep the getters, the package-private methods that maintain both sides of an association, and
> the read-only collection getters;
> Do not add unique constraints, equals/hashCode, configuration files, or queries.
> Return every class in full.

| 1 | Used `javax.persistence` (outdated pre-Jakarta EE 9 namespace), which fails to compile on Jakarta EE 10. | Switch package imports to `jakarta.persistence`. |

Both issues were declared by the generator in its own list of decisions. The namespace
choice would have broken the build; it was caught by reading that list rather than by compiling.

### Decisions made by the generator, accepted

- `Long` as the identifier type. No type was specified, and it is the conventional choice for a
  JPA identity column.
- A surrogate key rather than promoting one of the business keys (`fiscalCode`, `healthNum`,
  `packCode`, `code`) to be the primary key.
- No `@Table`, `@Column` or `@JoinColumn` anywhere: all table and column names are left to the
  defaults.
- The enums left as plain Java enums with no annotations of their own; the annotation belongs
  on the field that uses them.
